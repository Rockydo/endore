# Known Issues

- The M0 thumbnail is a temporary vanilla-derived composition and will be replaced through
  the reference-driven image pipeline before the M11 art gate.
- The installed engine requires its 2,086 culture ABI keys to have populations. M4 keeps
  one distributed size `0.01` compatibility presence per custom-dominant land location;
  these technical presences never dominate and are not visible worldbuilding.
- Installed graphical-culture tags and the M2/M3 government, ruler, census, settlement,
  and raw-material scaffolds remain temporary. M5, M6, and M11 own those replacements;
  none is accepted for release.
