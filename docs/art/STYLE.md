# ENDÓRË generated-art contract

No generated art is required before the map and world gates. When an art milestone begins,
each asset family must use both of these reference sets:

1. Precise installed Europa Universalis V assets of the same asset class, dimensions,
   alpha behavior, framing, material rendering, and UI role. These are the binding style
   and format references.
2. Actual stills or official imagery from Peter Jackson's *The Fellowship of the Ring*,
   *The Two Towers*, and *The Return of the King* selected for the relevant place, people,
   costume, architecture, landscape, lighting, and mood.

Film references are art direction, not canon authority. Source material remains governed
by the canon precedence in the master plan. Downloaded references live only in a
gitignored cache on `G:\`; no film frame is committed or redistributed.

Every generation record must identify the exact vanilla and film references supplied.
Avoid generic fantasy, clip-art geometry, muddy yellow grading, and indiscriminate warm
filters. Match EU5's readable silhouettes, controlled palette, surface detail, edge
treatment, and presentation at final UI size.

Icons are generated as four genuinely different designs on one sheet, then split with
local tools. Each child asset is checked for crop, alpha, dimensions, edge quality,
small-size readability, uniqueness, and visual agreement with its references. Failed
children are regenerated as another four-up sheet; no new audio is generated.
