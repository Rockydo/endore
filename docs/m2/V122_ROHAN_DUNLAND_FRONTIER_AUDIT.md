# v122 Rohan-Dunland frontier audit

## Scope

This M2 slice resolves the one deliberately-open positive control at the Fords of Isen
and locks the named Rohirric anchors immediately relevant to the Dunland/Isengard
frontier. It changes no location owner in the current world: all four witnesses already
resolve to Rohan. It does not redraw Dunland, enlarge Isengard, alter the Gap, or change
any coast, terrain, forest, or river.

## Required TA 3018 controls

| Witness | Required owner | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fords of Isen | Rohan | *The Two Towers*, Book III Ch. 7: the Fords are a Rohirric Westfold strongpoint before Saruman's 3019 assault. |
| Helm's Deep | Rohan | *The Two Towers*, Book III Ch. 7: the Hornburg/Helm's Deep is the fortress of the Westfold. |
| Edoras | Rohan | *The Two Towers*, Book III Ch. 6: the royal seat of the Mark. |
| Dunharrow | Rohan | *The Return of the King*, Book V Ch. 3: the Rohirric refuge and muster place. |

## Consequence

The former negative-only Fords safeguard is removed. A future allocator must now retain
Rohan at the exact source-resolved Fords location, while the compact Nan Curunir contract
continues to restrict Isengard to its source-reviewed ring-domain. This is a named-site
control audit, not evidence for a cadastral Rohan-Dunland line: uncertain surrounding Gap
locations retain their existing source-bound or wild status.
